Game Name:
LettersFall 110%

Keywords:
Tetris, Scrabble, free, fun, open-source, word, spelling, game, Flatpak, Linux

Short Description:
Educational free MIT open-source American English word spelling game for Linux.

Long Description:
When you combine the video game Tetris with the board game Scrabble you get this game.  Letters rapidly fall and you must correctly spell American English words to clear them.  Features nice graphics, good sound effects, and a great music soundtrack.  Also has easy to play mouse controls and fun and challenging game play.  Plus multiple game modes with varying difficulty and a saved high score table for each mode. Features a 450,000+ word American English dictionary.  Do you have the skills to battle your way to the end and win?  "Learn To Have Fun!"
